## Semantic changes worth recording

### The CCO workflow's MCC source moved from a data table to Postgres

`Get Elavon MCC` was deleted from
[[../workflows/elavon-bi-edit-merchant-application-and-send-cco-enrollment]]; the MCC now
comes from `merchant.data->'business_type'->>'mcc_sic'` in the eligibility query.
[[../resources/data-tables/eefxih6vfsgkmssi|Elavon - MIDs]] was ~6 months stale
(`Last_Sync` 2025-11-11 .. 2026-03-14) and missing 2 of the eligible merchants, including
the only one with a retrievable signed document.

Measured trade-off, recorded because it may need revisiting: the two sources genuinely
disagree for **271 of 2,460 merchants (~11%)** after excluding staleness-explained cases,
and 381 of 413 disagreements resolve to a *different* commodity code. Across the 91
CCO-eligible merchants they agreed 13/13. Detail in
[[../resources/databases/postgres-bdw1qodl0v7mywj6]].

### Correction to a previously recorded fact

[[../resources/databases/postgres-bdw1qodl0v7mywj6]] previously claimed
`merchant.processor_merchant_id` "also carries" the Elavon MID. **It does not** — that
column is null for all 91 CCO-eligible merchants. `merchant_processor_detail` is the only
reliable source, and it must be filtered by `processor_id = 'elavon'` and
`~ '^[0-9]+$'`. The note has been corrected.
